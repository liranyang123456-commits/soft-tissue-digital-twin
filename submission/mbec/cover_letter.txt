Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript entitled "From Endoscopic Image Sequences
to a Simulable Soft-Tissue Digital Twin: Gaussian Surface Tracking and
Force-Scale-Aware Mechanical Inversion" as an Original Research Article in
Medical & Biological Engineering & Computing.

The manuscript proposes a complete observation-to-simulation framework:
endoscopic images and auxiliary depth are converted into a canonical
Gaussian surface and optical material representation; fixed primitive
identities preserve deformation correspondence; an explicit
Gaussian-to-tetrahedral map transfers surface observations to
differentiable Neo-Hookean mechanics; and the resulting model predicts
deformation, stress, and strain under prescribed loads. A force-scale
analysis separates absolute-modulus uncertainty from force-scale-invariant
stiffness contrast, while a provenance map records whether each parameter
is measured, estimated, or assumed.

The evaluation combines two public endoscopic sequences, a complete
60-scenario finite-element campaign, common-protocol reconstruction
baselines, a physical ultrasound phantom with independent compression-test
measurements, and a patient-specific tetrahedral liver geometry generated
from de-identified hospital computed-tomography images. Source code,
evaluation protocols, and machine-readable result summaries are available
at https://github.com/liranyang123456-commits/soft-tissue-digital-twin.

The common reconstruction holdout is reported without a
state-of-the-art claim: the present representation is less accurate than
EndoGaussian because it prioritizes fixed mechanical correspondence. The
complete finite-element campaign reports a 9.6% median background-modulus
error and identifies weak recovery of deep-inclusion properties. We
believe this transparent separation of visual fidelity, mechanical
identifiability, and parameter provenance is directly relevant to MBEC's
readership.

The manuscript is original, has not been published, and is not under
consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission.
