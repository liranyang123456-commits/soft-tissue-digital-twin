Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript entitled "Audited Soft-Tissue Digital Twins from Monocular Endoscopic Video" as a Research article in BioMedical Engineering OnLine.

The manuscript presents an engineering pipeline that converts a monocular endoscopic video into a simulable soft-tissue digital twin and labels every physical parameter as measured, estimated, or assumed. A force-scale result shows that stiffness contrast remains reliable when the absolute force scale is uncertain. The study uses de-identified clinical computed-tomography images from Henan Provincial People's Hospital, a physical ultrasound phantom with independent compression-test measurements, and public endoscopic and finite-element datasets.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has not been published. All authors have approved this submission.

Code, evaluation scripts, and the data-split definitions are available at https://github.com/liranyang123456-commits/soft-tissue-digital-twin. The de-identified hospital computed-tomography images are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request.
