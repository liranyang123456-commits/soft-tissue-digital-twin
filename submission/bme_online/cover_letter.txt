Please consider our manuscript entitled "Audited Soft-Tissue Digital Twins from Monocular Endoscopic Video" as a Research article in BioMedical Engineering OnLine.

Based on 3D Gaussian splatting and differentiable Neo-Hookean finite
elements, the manuscript presents an audited soft-tissue digital twin from
monocular endoscopic video. Reconstruction, deformation tracking, and
mechanical inversion act on the same Gaussian primitives. A force-scale
analysis separates absolute-modulus uncertainty from force-scale-invariant
stiffness contrast, and a provenance map records whether each parameter is
measured, estimated, or assumed. The evaluation combines public
endoscopic data, a 60-scenario finite-element benchmark, a physical
ultrasound phantom with compression-test measurements, and a
patient-specific liver mesh generated from de-identified clinical
computed tomography images from Henan Provincial People's Hospital.

The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has not been published. All authors have approved this submission.

Code, evaluation scripts, and the data-split definitions are available at https://github.com/liranyang123456-commits/soft-tissue-digital-twin. The de-identified hospital computed tomography images are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request.
